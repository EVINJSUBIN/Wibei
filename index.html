Quick Start Guide
01
Search a song
Type any song name in the search bar above. Suggestions appear as you type — just click one to play.
02
Paste a link
Got a YouTube or Spotify URL? Paste it in the link box. Wibei will stream it directly — no download needed.
03
Pick a visualizer
Once music is playing, use the top bar to switch between Spectrum, Pulse Ring, Wave, Crystal, and Grid styles.
04
Change theme
Try different color themes in the top bar — Cyan, Neon Pink, Gold, or Minimal White. They update live while playing.
05
Upload or use mic
Click "Upload audio file" to visualize a local file, or "Use microphone" to visualize real-time audio from your environment.
06
Watch the beat
Wibei detects bass hits and drops in real time. The screen flashes, art pulses, and the background reacts on every beat.
