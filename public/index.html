// REAL-TIME AUDIO PROCESSING

The Sound Shaper DSP Rack

Direct Web Audio API node graph routing in your browser with zero latency. No proprietary software required.

Underwater Lowpass Muffler

Emulates the iconic "club bathroom / submerged underwater" vibe. Dynamically activates a 650Hz BiquadFilterNode with a resonant Q of 1.2 to smoothly filter high frequencies.

NODE: BiquadFilterNode (Lowpass)

Bass Overdrive Boost

A low-shelf equalizer with +10dB gain at 120Hz. Drives low-end saturation and supercharges the visualizer's spring physics and mesh displacement.

NODE: BiquadFilterNode (Lowshelf)

Speed & Pitch Modulator

Switch between Normal (1.0x), Nightcore (1.25x high pitch), and Slowed Vibe (0.85x warm detuned tempo) with instantaneous clock reconfiguration.

ENGINE: HTMLMediaElement.playbackRate

Beat Drop Shockwaves

Real-time transient energy monitoring detecting sub-bass spikes. Spawns 3D expanding shockwave particle rings across the floor on heavy drops.

TRIGGER: FFT Transient Peak > 0.55

Universal Audio Ingest

Drop any .mp3 or .wav anywhere on the screen, tap your live microphone to visualize real-world acoustics, or search global tracks with 0ms buffering.

PIPELINE: Drag & Drop / WebRTC Mic

Auto-Pilot Drone Cam

A dynamic cinematic camera choreography mode that smoothly orbits, angles, and zooms around your visualizer in sync with music intensity.

CONTROLLER: Three.js Orbit Choreography

// OUR MISSION & VISION

"Audio visualization shouldn't require bloated desktop software. It should be open, instantaneous, and accessible to every hacker, producer, and curious mind."

Wibei was built from scratch as an open-source web synthesizer for Hack Club Stardance. It exists to prove that cutting-edge 3D WebGL graphics and real-time DSP sound filters can live directly in a modern web browser.

Whether you are testing an acoustic mix, performing a live DJ set with the microphone input, or simply chilling with lofi beats after a late-night coding session, Wibei is designed to make sound feel alive.

100% OPEN SOURCE • Built with Three.js & Web Audio API
