wibei

3D audio spectrograph & DSP lab

[ launch studio ] [ source code ]

Built for Hack Club Stardance by @EVINJSUBIN and @aamosm.


Route lossless audio, experiment with real-time DSP filter nodes (biquad), and render acoustic harmonics in 3D space with zero latency.

Engines

  • Radial Pulse: 128 towers scaling radially with sub-bass kick transients & center hologram.
  • Waveform: 64-channel linear frequency analyzer & kinetic ribbon highway.
  • Matrix: 16x16 spatial voxel grid pulsing to harmonic shockwaves.
  • Pulsar Sphere: Geodesic icosahedron deformed by harmonic bins with dual gimbal rings.

Aesthetic Vibes

  • Serious Void: Distraction-free monochrome mixing spectrograph (default).
  • Phonk / Drift: Blood Crimson & Toxic Violet neon bloom with heavy bass physics.
  • Comic / Pop: High-contrast tri-color palette with comic vibrancy.
  • Lofi Sunset: Peach, Lavender & Dusk atmosphere.
  • Cyber Matrix: Acid green phosphors with digital terminal feel.
  • Dynamic Auto: Real-time FFT mood engine that shifts the entire app with the music.
