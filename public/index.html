Getting Started

Welcome to Wibei

🎵

1. Play Audio Any Way You Like

Search any song on YouTube, click one of the Quick Play demos, toggle your Microphone (🎤), or drop in a local .mp3 file (📁).

2. Interactive 3D Camera Controls

Click & drag anywhere on the canvas to freely rotate the visualizer in 3D space. Move your cursor to see smooth camera parallax tilting.

3. Customize Visualizer & Glow

Switch between Ring, Wave, and Matrix Grid modes, toggle the Synth Grid floor, or switch themes (Gold, Cyan, Pink).

Keyboard Shortcuts

1 / 2 / 3 Switch Visualizer Style
R Reset 3D Camera Angle
F Toggle Fullscreen
