// ARCHITECTURE & PROCESSING

The Sound Shaper DSP Suite

Engineered directly on the browser's native Web Audio API graph for zero-latency filter processing.

Submerged Lowpass Muffler

Instantly triggers a resonant lowpass filter with a cutoff threshold at 450Hz and resonant Q factor of 2.5. Delivers the legendary "club bathroom / underwater soundstage" atmosphere without touching any external software.

NODE GRAPH: BiquadFilterNode (Lowpass, fc=450Hz, Q=2.5)

Analog Bass Boost

A dedicated low-shelf EQ node boosting sub-bass frequencies below 140Hz by +16dB, physically driving 3D bar height scaling.

NODE: BiquadFilterNode (+16dB Gain)

Speed Clock Modulation

Real-time sample rate pitching between 1.0x Normal, 1.25x Nightcore, and 0.85x Slowed Reverb tempo.

API: HTMLMediaElement.playbackRate

Beat Drop Shockwaves

Sub-bass transient detector calculating kinetic acoustic energy spikes to trigger expanding 3D shockwave particle rings.

PHYSICS: Transient Peak Energy > 0.55

Auto-Pilot Drone Cam

Dynamic orbital camera choreography analyzing frequency energy to pan, zoom, and tilt automatically around 3D meshes.

CHOREOGRAPHY: Three.js Dynamic Orbit
