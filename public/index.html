getting started

Play a demo

Click any track in the Demos tab on the left — they load instantly.

Search YouTube

Switch to the Search tab and type any song name. Note: YouTube occasionally blocks cloud server requests. If that happens, use a demo or upload a file.

Upload or mic

Drop in any .mp3 or .wav file, or click Use microphone to visualize live audio.

3D controls

Click and drag to rotate the visualizer. Change shape and color from the right panel.
